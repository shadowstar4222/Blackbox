BLACKBOX 2.0 NATIVE PREVIEW
Current release: 2.0.0-preview.17

1. Extract the complete Windows x64 ZIP.
2. Run Blackbox.App.exe. OBS and an external video player are not needed.
3. Start your game or application and leave its window open.
4. In Games, select the real running window, choose Remember, then Capture.
5. Press Record, or Enable auto for remembered applications.
6. In Microphone, choose routing and a voice preset, then Apply.
7. In Recordings, review a continuous session, add markers, or export one video.

Settings contains recording quality, Start with Windows, automatic watching,
and Close to notification area. To fully exit, use Blackbox's tray menu.

The optional Diagnostics recording-setup window is a capturable test scene with
music and recording-settings overlays. Its bright colors and motion may trigger
seizures or discomfort. Read and confirm its warning before starting.

IMPORTANT PREVIEW LIMITS
Windows 10 captures system-output audio plus the microphone into one stereo mix;
other audible applications can be included. Isolated tracks are not available.
Hardware encoding is requested but may fall back to software. Capture-blocking
games are not bypassed. Time-shift buffers completed segments, not live frames.
Test under your normal workload before relying on a long recording.

Recordings: %USERPROFILE%\Videos\Blackbox\Application\YYYY-MM-DD
Settings and logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Blackbox

Keep the full portable folder together. Exit Blackbox before replacing it.
Settings and recordings live outside that folder and should not be removed.
Keep superseded builds together in one clearly labeled deletable folder.
