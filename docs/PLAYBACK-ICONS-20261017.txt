BLACKBOX CLEARER REVIEW CONTROLS
October 17, 2026 - 2.0.0-preview.18 - local testing build

The review transport no longer uses ambiguous ASCII bar/arrow combinations.
Start/end use standard media-boundary icons; segment jumps use a filmstrip;
single-frame steps show one outlined frame with a direction arrow and "1";
10-second jumps show curved direction arrows and "10s". The larger 36x34 hit
targets retain Blackbox's dark surfaces, focus border, and interaction states.
The central Play/Pause button uses the existing cyan accent.

Hover descriptions name the exact action. Screen-reader names follow those
descriptions, and Play/Pause updates both its icon and action name. Button
handlers, keyboard shortcuts, capture, seeking, and decoder behavior are unchanged.
No new icon/font dependency, raster downloads, or animation timers were added.
Core glyphs use the existing Segoe MDL2 Assets family supplied with Windows;
the frame and filmstrip details are lightweight WPF layout primitives.
Glyph reference: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/iconography/segoe-ui-symbol-font

Validation: artifacts/playback-icons-20261017. Nine new source regressions cover
the eight seek-handler/template pairs plus theme inheritance and accessible
names. Packaged WPF checks validate all nine buttons, glyph availability, hit
targets, accessible names, non-overlap with audio/speed options, and nonblank
toolbar snapshots at both normal and compact widths. The existing native video
decode, forward/reverse frame-step, paused-frame stability, decoder reuse, and
seek-coalescing checks are retained. No live recording or microphone capture
is required; playback uses isolated neutral fixture media with muted output.

PASSED
378 main tests and 54 native-capture tests (432 total), the packaged recorder
probe, and full WPF page/playback regressions at 1240x780 and 1024x680. Transport
screenshots at 1048- and 832-pixel page widths passed pixel-content checks and
visual inspection. Seek actions fit without overlap and retained their labels.
