BLACKBOX UI CLEANUP
October 17, 2026 - 2.0.0-preview.11

The left sidebar is the single visible recording/watching/readiness indicator.
The current window name remains on Capture and in the sidebar status tooltip.

Removed:
- Duplicate footer status and its indicator dot.
- Recorder-ready and repeated window-name header badges.
- Routine watching/automation text, idle health states and repeated target name.
- Generic page subtitles and navigation-rail category/descriptor text.
- No-live-meter / waiting-for-video placeholders when telemetry is unavailable.
- Redundant links to Microphone and Diagnostics beside the permanent navigation.
- The second back arrow in review and the permanent Local playback label.
- Double dividers in Games and Recordings, and the unused status-badge style.

Preserved:
- Capture warnings, repair/fault information and action errors.
- Video dimensions/FPS, audio meter when available, and expanded fault evidence.
- Time-shift/follow state in review.
- Review back navigation, recording controls, quality settings and Help/tutorial.
- Full state information in Diagnostics, where it is useful for troubleshooting.
- Recording-setup overlay settings and warnings, which are intentionally recorded.
